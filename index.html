01ABOUT

Sparrow are a young four-piece from Adelaide playing loud, raw, experimental grunge. Punk attitude up front, with jazz and Australiana influences running underneath.

Will Fleming on vocals and guitar, Cameron Cowie on guitar, Nathan Ebbs on bass and Moses Portolesi on drums.
